Merchant Center price and availability errors: format vs storefront

By Rowvia · Published 22 October 2026 · Official Google guidance checked on this date

A price or stock warning can mean a malformed field or a disagreement with the actual offer. Fixing the text does not establish that customers can buy the product on those terms.

Check amount and currency separately

Google’s price guidance specifies a number with an ISO 4217 currency code. For a fictional USD offer, 19.99 USD has a plausible structure; it still needs comparison with the real amount and currency. A bare $19.99 leaves the explicit currency code missing.

Do not replace every currency with USD to remove a warning. Trace the amount and currency to the system that owns the offer, then repair its mapping. Price must agree with the landing page and checkout. Review target-country currency and tax rules in the linked guidance; this article does not cover regional or special pricing exceptions.

Choose stock status from the actual order policy

Google’s availability guidance documents these ordinary retail values:

  • in_stock: orders are accepted and can be fulfilled.
  • out_of_stock: the item cannot currently be purchased or orders are not accepted.
  • preorder: an unreleased product for which orders are accepted.
  • backorder: an existing unavailable product for which orders are accepted pending restock.

Preorder and backorder require availability_date, with the expected date also visible on the landing page. The documented build_to_order value is for vehicle ads, outside this retail guide.

“Available soon” is therefore a question for the merchant, not a safe automatic replacement. Confirm release status and whether orders are accepted before mapping it. Check the same variant’s stock status on the landing page and through checkout.

Use a two-pass review

  1. Mechanical pass: inspect the saved export for the amount/currency structure and supported availability value. Keep the original privately.
  2. Offer pass: open the exact submitted variant and compare the export with the selected option and checkout. A fictional blue mug may be in stock while the red option is sold out; the parent page’s headline alone does not resolve that difference.
  3. Reprocess: after correcting the source or mapping, inspect Merchant Center’s resulting messages. A clean format does not establish approval or resolve unrelated requirements.

Keep a private review category such as “currency mapping,” “stock-state mapping” or “storefront mismatch.” You do not need to share product records to describe the problem.
