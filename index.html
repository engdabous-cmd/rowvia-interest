Mechanical issues

Supported rule types include duplicate IDs, missing descriptions, malformed price fields and invalid GTIN check digits within a partial basic retail profile.

02 / REVIEW

Facts need a human

A mathematically valid GTIN does not prove manufacturer assignment. Missing or uncertain product facts need real evidence, not generated values.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Formatting has limits

The prototype has limited header, enum and currency case/spacing normalization. It does not invent identifiers or fill in missing product facts.

Before you express interest

Know the boundaries.

Rowvia is independent of Google. Google Merchant Center is a Google product; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

  • The prototype accepts comma-delimited UTF-8 CSV only, up to 2 MiB, 5,000 product records, 100 columns and 20,000 JavaScript code units per cell. TSV, XML, Excel and ZIP input are outside this scope.
  • CSV checking is a preparation step. Google’s product-file guidance documents tab-delimited text and XML. A checked CSV is not a certified direct-upload file; confirm the requirements for your submission route.
  • Rowvia does not inspect live pages, images, checkout, account diagnostics, shipping/tax policy or complete category/country requirements. It cannot promise Merchant Center approval or full compliance.
  • Browser-local processing is the design intent. Runtime privacy and accessibility verification remain incomplete. No privacy or security certification is claimed. No validator or file upload is available on this page.
